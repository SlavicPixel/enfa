What the model predicts

The model predicts whether a stock will make a significant price move of ±{{ threshold }}% or more the following trading day, regardless of direction. This is a binary classification task:

Volatile Large move expected tomorrow
Calm No significant move expected

Direction (UP or DOWN) is not predicted — experiments showed next-day direction is not reliably predictable from public data, consistent with the efficient market hypothesis.
